About IDX to MP4 Converter

IDX/SUB files consist of VobSub bitmap subtitle streams extracted from DVDs. Converting or muxing them into an MP4 container allows seamless playback on modern web browsers, mobile devices, and media players.

  • Supports soft-muxing or hardcoding bitmap subtitles.
  • Adjust output resolution and bitrate dynamically.
  • Client-side simulated processing workflow with instant preview.
